About This Resource

What is Speak & Click Lab?

Speak & Click Lab is a gamified digital educational resource designed to solve a critical classroom challenge: students show limited speaking opportunities, low confidence when expressing ideas orally, and a tendency to participate without producing spoken language in English.

Through a structured PPP (Presentation, Practice, Production) approach combined with guided speaking techniques, students progress from visual recognition to independent oral production — building confidence with every interaction.

  • Students identify and describe colors and classroom objects using short oral sentences
  • Interactive challenges move learners from recognition to speaking gradually
  • Immediate feedback reinforces correct answers and encourages participation
  • Space theme creates an immersive, motivating environment for exploration

Learning Structure

The 4 Mission Zones

Each zone follows the PPP methodology — from visual comprehension to guided practice to independent speaking production.

01
🔭 Zone 1 · Presentation

EXPLORE — Visual Input

Students observe and recognize vocabulary through visual stimuli — no speaking pressure here. Build recognition before production.

  • Watch short videos about colors and objects
  • Observe images and digital flashcards
  • Click and select correct colors
  • Match images to vocabulary
Goal: Comprehension without speaking pressure
02
🛸 Zone 2 · Practice

INTERACT — Guided Practice

Students interact with digital tools through structured activities. Repetition builds familiarity and reduces anxiety.

  • Matching and memory games
  • Drag-and-drop exercises
  • Sorting objects by color
  • True/false identification tasks
Goal: Guided participation + repetition
03
🎤 Zone 3 · Production

EXPRESS — Guided Speaking

Students speak with structured support using sentence frames. Confidence grows through repetition and positive reinforcement.

  • Repeat after audio model
  • Use sentence frames: "It is red"
  • Complete "The ___ is ___" structures
  • Respond to "What color is it?"
Goal: Supported speaking production
04
🌠 Zone 4 · Output

REFLECT — Independent Output

Students describe what they see or create, drawing objects and explaining them orally. Maximum speaking confidence!

  • Draw space objects using the digital canvas
  • Describe drawings orally using learned vocabulary
  • Answer simple questions independently
  • Create and present their space scene
Goal: Independent speaking + confidence

Pedagogical Notes

For Teachers

🎯

Learning Objective

Students will be able to identify and describe colors and classroom objects using short oral sentences, showing increased confidence in speaking.

  • Recognize basic colors: red, blue, yellow, green
  • Name classroom objects: pencil, book, chair, bag
  • Produce: "It is ___" and "The ___ is ___"
📚

PPP Methodology

This resource follows the Presentation → Practice → Production approach combined with guided speaking techniques.

  • Zone 1: Presentation (visual input, flashcards)
  • Zone 2: Practice (matching, games, repetition)
  • Zone 3: Production (guided speaking with frames)
  • Zone 4: Output (independent description)
💬

Feedback Strategy

Positive reinforcement is prioritized over direct correction. Interactive platforms offer immediate digital feedback.

  • Repeat and model rather than correct
  • Immediate right/wrong visual feedback
  • Stars and streaks motivate participation
  • Zone 3 self-assessment builds agency
👥

Target Audience

Designed for beginner English learners at the Pre-A1 CEFR level in a virtual or hybrid classroom environment.

  • Ages 6–12 (primary/elementary)
  • Pre-A1 → A1 CEFR level
  • Virtual and hybrid classrooms
  • Low speaking confidence learners
🎲

Gamified Elements

The resource integrates gamification to maintain engagement and reduce speaking anxiety.

  • Star points for correct answers
  • Streak counter for motivation
  • Progress tracking across activities
  • Quiz challenges with scoring
🛠️

Sentence Structures

Two core structures are practiced throughout all zones to build oral production gradually.

  • "It is ___." (colors)
  • "The ___ is ___." (objects + colors)
  • "What color is it?" (Q&A)
  • "What is this?" (identification)
